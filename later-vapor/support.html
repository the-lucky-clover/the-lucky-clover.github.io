Support

Questions about flying, purchases, or privacy? We’re here to help.

Contact support

Email theluckyclover@gmail.com.

For technical problems, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a description of what happened. Please don’t send your Apple Account password or payment details.

Controls

Tap Start on the title screen. Use the virtual stick to steer; pull it down to climb and push it up to descend. Use the cannon and missile buttons to fire. Missiles require a target lock.

Remove ads

The iOS edition is planned as a free game with ads, with an optional one-time Remove ads purchase. Its planned U.S. price is $2.99; the App Store will show the local price before you confirm.

Restore a purchase

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
  2. Open Later Vapor and choose Restore purchases on the title screen.
  3. If the purchase is not found, check your connection and try again. Contact us if you still need help.

Privacy options

When available for your region, select Privacy options on the title screen or while paused to review your advertising choices. Read our privacy policy for more information.
