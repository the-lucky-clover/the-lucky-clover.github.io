Privacy policy

How the Later Vapor iOS app handles information and how to contact us about privacy.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Information stored on your device

The game stores your best score locally on your device. Deleting the app may delete that score. The app does not require you to create a game account.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free app uses Google AdMob to display ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices. Google’s advertising SDK may collect or receive information such as your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers where available, ads you have seen, interactions with the app or ads, and diagnostic and performance information. Google and participating ad providers may use this information to deliver and measure ads, improve their services, and, depending on your choices and settings, personalize advertising.

The app may show a consent message depending on your location and the message configured for the app. When a privacy options button is available on the title screen or while paused, you can use it to review or change your choices. Your device and Google account may also provide advertising controls. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services and read Google’s privacy policy.

iOS may ask whether you allow tracking across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising. You can change that choice in Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. If you decline, Google’s advertising SDK does not send the Apple advertising identifier in ad requests; the app also requests restricted processing and non-personalized ads.

If you purchase Remove ads, the app checks your Apple purchase entitlement and stops displaying ads while that entitlement is active. The consent service may still be contacted to determine applicable privacy choices.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit to check the Remove ads purchase and restore it when requested. We do not receive your payment card details through the app. Apple’s handling of App Store information is described in App Store & Privacy.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use those messages to respond and keep support records. Please avoid sending passwords or payment details.

Your requests

For questions or requests about information you have sent to us, email theluckyclover@gmail.com. Google and Apple handle information under their own policies; their linked policies explain their available privacy controls and request processes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its services change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
